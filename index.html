Cairn — Support

Cairn is a private, offline reflective journal for medical students. It runs entirely on your device, collects no data, and makes no network requests.

Get help — open an issue

Common questions

I forgot my passphrase. Use the one-time recovery key shown when you created your journal — tap “Forgot passphrase?” on the lock screen and enter it. If you didn’t save your recovery key and don’t remember your passphrase, your journal cannot be recovered (that’s by design — no one, including us, can read it).

Where is my data? Only on your device, encrypted. Cairn has no account and no server — turn on Airplane Mode and it still works. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I move my journal to a new device? Settings → Back up creates an encrypted backup you can move via the Files app or a share sheet; on the new device, use Import backup.

How do I delete everything? Settings → Erase all data on this device. Because nothing is stored off-device, deleting the app (and any backups you made) removes your data entirely.
