Cairn — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-07-03 · Publisher: Backshear LLC

Cairn is a private, offline reflective journal for medical students. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information. In short: Cairn collects nothing, sends nothing, and stores everything encrypted on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Cairn has no analytics, no accounts, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. Backshear LLC receives no data from your use of the app — we cannot see your journal, your usage, your device, or anything else.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you write stays on your device only, inside an encrypted vault:

  • Your entries are encrypted with a key derived from your passphrase (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256, 600,000 iterations) and a random 256-bit master key, using AES-GCM-256. The vault uses iOS Complete File Protection and is excluded from iCloud and device backups.
  • If you turn on Face ID / Touch ID unlock, the key that unlocks your journal is wrapped by a non-exportable key held in this device’s Secure Enclave; it never leaves the device.
  • A recovery key (which you save yourself, off-device) can also open the vault if you forget your passphrase. Cairn never stores your recovery key.
  • An optional, non-secret passphrase hint is stored in the clear so it can appear on the lock screen. Do not put anything sensitive in it.
  • An optional username you may set is a display-only label for your device’s sign-in / passkey prompt. It is stored on your device, is never treated as patient data, and is never included in any export.

Backups and exports you create are written only where you choose (e.g., the Files app or a share-sheet destination you pick). Encrypted backups remain encrypted; de-identified text/PDF exports are produced locally.

Microphone & speech (optional dictation)

If you choose to dictate a note, Cairn uses the microphone and Apple’s on-device speech recognition, which is forced to run entirely on your device. Audio is transcribed locally and is never sent to Apple or any server, and the audio itself is discarded — only the text you keep is saved, encrypted, in your journal. Permission is requested only the first time you tap to dictate.

Network use

None. Cairn makes zero network requests. This is enforced structurally by a Content Security Policy (connect-src 'none') and by a navigation guard in the app shell. You can confirm it yourself by turning on Airplane Mode — the app works fully.

Protected Health Information (PHI)

Cairn is designed to hold no patient identifiers. The data model never asks for names, dates of birth, record numbers, addresses, phone numbers, emails, photos, or provider names; ages are recorded only as broad bands, and encounters carry no calendar dates. A built-in check assists you in catching identifiers, but it is an assist, not a guarantee — you are responsible for keeping your entries de-identified and for following your school’s and hospital’s policies, which may be stricter than the law. Cairn is not a covered entity’s system and is not, by itself, “HIPAA compliant”; HIPAA compliance is a property of an organization’s program, not of an app.

Children

Cairn is intended for adult medical students and is not directed to children.

Data deletion

You can erase all data at any time from Settings → Erase all data on this device. Because nothing is stored off-device, deleting the app (and any backups you made) removes your data entirely.

Your responsibility & disclaimer

Cairn is a personal reflective journal — not an electronic health record, not clinical decision support, and not a substitute for any patient-encounter log your institution requires.
