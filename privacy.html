Addendum — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-07-11 · Publisher: Backshear LLC

Addendum is a private, offline reflective journal for medical students. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information. In short: Addendum collects nothing, sends nothing, and stores everything encrypted on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Addendum has no analytics, no accounts, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. Backshear LLC receives no data from your use of the app — we cannot see your journal, your usage, your device, or anything else.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you write stays on your device only, inside an encrypted vault:

  • Your entries are encrypted with a key derived from your passphrase (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256, 600,000 iterations) and a random 256-bit master key, using AES-GCM-256. The vault uses iOS Complete File Protection and is excluded from device backups. It reaches iCloud only if you turn on the optional iCloud sync described below — and then only as the already-encrypted vault, which Apple cannot read.
  • If you turn on Face ID / Touch ID unlock, the key that unlocks your journal is wrapped by a non-exportable key held in this device’s Secure Enclave; it never leaves the device.
  • A recovery key (which you save yourself, off-device) can also open the vault if you forget your passphrase. Addendum never stores your recovery key.
  • An optional, non-secret passphrase hint is stored in the clear so it can appear on the lock screen. Do not put anything sensitive in it.
  • An optional username you may set is a display-only label for your device’s sign-in / passkey prompt. It is stored on your device, is never treated as patient data, and is never included in any export.

Backups and exports you create are written only where you choose (e.g., the Files app or a share-sheet destination you pick). Encrypted backups remain encrypted; de-identified text/PDF exports are produced locally.

Microphone & speech (optional dictation)

If you choose to dictate a note, Addendum uses the microphone and Apple’s on-device speech recognition, which is forced to run entirely on your device. Audio is transcribed locally and is never sent to Apple or any server, and the audio itself is discarded — only the text you keep is saved, encrypted, in your journal. Permission is requested only the first time you tap to dictate.

Local reminders (optional, off by default)

If you turn on Settings → Reminders, Addendum schedules a small number of local notifications on your device — a nudge when your last backup is getting old, and a heads-up before a shelf exam you’ve dated in Pearls. These are scheduled entirely on this device (Apple’s local-notification system): there is no server, no push service, and nothing leaves your device. Notification text is deliberately generic — it never contains journal content, rotation names, or anything about a patient. The system permission prompt appears only when you turn the setting on, and turning it off (or erasing all data) removes every scheduled reminder. You can also verify the app’s build checksum and its live no-network policy at any time in Settings → Privacy you can verify (checksums are published on the Release checksums page).

Network use

None by default. Out of the box, Addendum makes zero network requests — enforced structurally by a Content Security Policy (connect-src 'none') and a navigation guard in the app shell. You can confirm it by turning on Airplane Mode; the app works fully.

iCloud sync (optional, off by default)

Addendum offers an optional iCloud sync you can turn on in Settings → iCloud sync. When it’s on:

  • Your journal syncs across your own Apple devices through your iCloud account (Apple’s CloudKit private database). We operate no server and never receive your data.
  • Only the already-encrypted vault is synced. Your entries are encrypted on your device with a key derived from your passphrase, which never leaves your devices — so Apple (and Backshear) only ever hold opaque ciphertext and cannot read your journal.
  • Use the same passphrase on each device. Your entries merge across devices — nothing is dropped. If you edit the same entry on two devices while both are offline, the later edit of that entry wins.
  • Turning sync off returns the app to fully offline. This is the only feature that uses the network.

Protected Health Information (PHI)

Addendum is designed to hold no patient identifiers. The data model never asks for names, dates of birth, record numbers, addresses, phone numbers, emails, photos, or provider names; ages are recorded only as broad bands, and encounters carry no calendar dates. A built-in check assists you in catching identifiers, but it is an assist, not a guarantee — you are responsible for keeping your entries de-identified and for following your school’s and hospital’s policies, which may be stricter than the law. Addendum is not a covered entity’s system and is not, by itself, “HIPAA compliant”; HIPAA compliance is a property of an organization’s program, not of an app.

Children

Addendum is intended for adult medical students and is not directed to children.

Data deletion

You can erase all data at any time from Settings → Erase all data on this device. Because nothing is stored off-device, deleting the app (and any backups you made) removes your data entirely.

Your responsibility & disclaimer
